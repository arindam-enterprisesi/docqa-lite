# Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees are reimbursed for reasonable business expenses incurred while working. Every claim must be submitted in the finance system within 30 days of the expense, with an itemised receipt attached.

Meals during business travel are reimbursed up to a daily limit of 50 USD (or local equivalent). Alcohol is not reimbursed. Client entertainment needs prior approval from a department head and must list the attendees.

Purchases of software or equipment above 500 USD require a purchase order instead of an expense claim. Claims without receipts, or submitted after 30 days, are rejected. Finance pays approved claims in the next monthly payroll run.
