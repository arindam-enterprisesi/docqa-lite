# Business Travel Policy

All business travel must be approved by your manager before booking. Flights, hotels and trains are booked through the company travel portal so that travel can be tracked for duty of care.

Economy class is standard for flights under six hours; premium economy may be booked for longer flights. Hotels should not exceed the city rate limit shown in the travel portal. Use public transport or shared taxis from airports where practical.

Travel insurance is provided automatically for trips booked through the portal. In an emergency while travelling, call the 24-hour travel assistance number printed on the back of your badge. Daily meal limits follow the expense reimbursement policy.
