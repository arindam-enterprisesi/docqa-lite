# New Joiner Onboarding Guide

On your first day you will receive your laptop, badge and accounts from the IT service desk at 9:30 am. Your manager will introduce you to your onboarding buddy, who is your first contact for everyday questions during your first month.

During week one, complete the mandatory training modules: security awareness, data privacy and the code of conduct. Set up multi-factor authentication before you access any internal system.

By the end of your first month you should have agreed your 90-day goals with your manager. A check-in meeting is held at 30, 60 and 90 days to review progress and collect your feedback about the onboarding experience.
